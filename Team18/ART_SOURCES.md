Create a bright, finely painted cozy indoor room in soft 2.5D mobile game illustration, eye level from a low camera facing the back wall. A large sky-blue trimmed window in the upper left shows a bright blue sky and small white clouds. Clear diagonal shafts of warm sunshine enter through the window and form bright windowpane reflections on the honey-oak plank floor. Use a bright pale sky-blue wall, a lilac detail or two, white trim, and natural warm oak for the floor. The floor starts about halfway down the frame and fills the foreground. Keep the central floor around 50% of the width and 73% of the height empty and sunlit, with plenty of room to overlay a small animated rabbit later. Use only a few small furnishings at the far edges; keep the main room spacious. Tall portrait composition around 9:16, composed so cropping the side margins for a taller mobile screen still preserves the main window and sunlit floor. Render only the room, with no rabbit, other animals, people, rugs in the central floor area, text, icons, graphics overlay, borders, logo or watermark.
